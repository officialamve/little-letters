About this beta

Little Letters v4.0 — English + Indian language starters. Speech uses your device. Not a full playschool replacement.

Age tip: ages 3–4 → Letters, Colours, Count. Ages 5–6 → Spell, Digraphs, Quiz, Stories.

Install: in Chrome, menu → Add to Home Screen for an app-like icon.
